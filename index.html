About the clinic

Comprehensive dental care, close to home.

Varsha Dental Clinic is a professional and affordable private dental practice offering an extensive range of services including cosmetic, orthodontic, periodontic, endodontic, implant and teeth whitening treatments. We provide team based, comprehensive dental care for children and adults, including patients of all ages who are anxious or have an underlying medical condition that adds complexity to their oral health.

  • General Dentistry: preventative and restorative care.
  • Orthodontic care to straighten crooked teeth in children and adults.
  • Gum and implant treatment for diseases of the gums and tooth replacement.

We are committed to helping you maintain oral health as an integral part of your overall wellness.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions

Quick answers about visiting Varsha Dental Clinic, our hours, procedures and pricing.

Where is Varsha Dental Clinic located?

Varsha Dental Clinic is at BL/010 Faizabad Road, next to VMart and opposite K.D. Singh Babu Stadium, Dayanand Nagar, Civil Lines, Barabanki (U.P.) 225001.

What are the clinic hours?

The clinic is open from 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. The clinic is closed every Thursday.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by calling or sending a WhatsApp message to +91 90264 77800. Walk-ins are also welcome during clinic hours.

Is root canal treatment (RCT) painful?

No. Root canal treatment at Varsha Dental Clinic is performed under local anaesthesia using modern equipment, so the procedure is painless and most patients return to normal activities the same day.

Does the clinic treat children?

Yes. Dr. Mahendra Verma provides pediatric dentistry including first dental visits, cavity treatment, fluoride application and habit counselling, with a child-friendly approach for anxious young patients.

Are the instruments properly sterilised?

Yes. The clinic follows strict hygiene protocols with autoclaved instruments, single-use essentials and hospital-grade sterilisation before every appointment.

Who is the dentist at Varsha Dental Clinic?

The clinic is led by Dr. Mahendra Verma, BDS, a dental surgeon with over a decade of experience in cosmetic, restorative and preventive dentistry, and the founder of Varsha Dental Clinic.

What dental services are offered?

Services include root canal treatment, dental implants, ultrasonic scaling, teeth whitening, crowns and bridges, dentures (RPD and CD), smile designing, pyorrhea treatment, oral rehabilitation, digital X-ray (RVG), pediatric dentistry, painless extractions and tooth jewellery.

How much does a dental consultation cost?

The clinic follows transparent, fair pricing and shares a full cost estimate before any treatment begins. For current consultation and procedure fees, contact +91 90264 77800 on WhatsApp.
